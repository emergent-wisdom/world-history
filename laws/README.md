The files the laws pass of 27 and 28 October wrote beside the construction. Its notes in the graph cite them by the
paths they had while the pass ran, under `.local-work/laws/`:

| File here | Cited as | What it is |
|---|---|---|

The laws themselves, the processes they derive and their values are in the construction. The notes that recorded
them are `note.laws-pass.phase-1` and the notes after it, held by laws-recorder, and the agents' notes are
`claude.laws-phase-1.*`, `claude.laws-corrections.*` and their closing notes.

The ledgers are published as their agents wrote them, with their notes for a next phase. Some of what they say was
done after them:
- Revisions 552 to 563 corrected the first ledger's findings, the growth gap through its first-ranked explanation,
  the crises the model does not hold. The surplus bound and the size of Çatalhöyük are held. What the corrections
  left open is listed at the end of the corrections ledger.
- The derived values the corrections ledger calls stale were derived again in revision 564, and the processes it
  says pass over Events were given readings there. The notes `note.laws-pass.after-sourced-corrections` and
  `note.laws-pass.after-sourced-corrections.reading` record that, and how to read the agreements that hold by
  construction.
- The corrections ledger says world output at 549 was scaled so that output per person holds. It was scaled from an
  interpolated value, so output per person held at what the old count gave, not at its own values; and the raised
  count at 549 raised the count read at 610, where output stayed. World output as people times output per person
  failed by 4.7% at 549 and 4.5% at 610. Revision 566 sets output at both dates to the product, the continents in
  proportion (`note.laws-pass.output-549-610`).
